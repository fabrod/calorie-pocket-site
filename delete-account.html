Delete your account

You can delete your Calorie Pocket account from inside the app, or contact us if you cannot sign in.

Effective and last updated: October 26, 2026

Delete from the app

  1. Open Calorie Pocket and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. If you want to stop paid renewal, choose Account → Manage / cancel and cancel in the store first.
  3. Go to Account → Delete account.
  4. Read the explanation, type DELETE, and choose Permanently delete account.
  5. Wait for the deletion-success message. If an error appears, deletion is not confirmed; retry or contact support.

If you used Apple login, separately remove Calorie Pocket under your Apple Account’s Sign in with Apple settings after deletion. The current app does not automatically revoke that separate Apple authorization.

What is removed and what may remain

The deletion flow removes your active authentication account, cloud nutrition profile, meals and deletion markers, AI usage records, and requests deletion of your RevenueCat customer profile. It clears that account’s local cache on the device performing deletion.

Separate guest logs remain on your devices. Offline copies on other devices, photos in your library, summaries shared with others, provider backup/security records, and legally required store payment records are not all erased by this action. See the retention explanation. Delete local app data on other devices if needed.

Deleting the account or app does not cancel an Apple/Google subscription. Manage renewal in the relevant store.

Cannot sign in?

Email fabrod035@gmail.com with the subject “Calorie Pocket account deletion request.” Send from your account email if possible, or explain that you used Apple’s private relay address. We will verify ownership before deleting account data. Do not send a password, login code, payment-card number, or identity document.

You can also contact us for a copy or correction of account information. Requests remain subject to applicable legal retention and verification requirements.
