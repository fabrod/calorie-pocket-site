Delete your account

  1. Open Calorie Pocket and sign in to the account you want to remove.
  2. Manage or cancel any store membership renewal separately; deletion does not cancel a subscription.
  3. Open Account → Delete account, read the disclosure and type DELETE.
  4. In the updated reports release, Apple users confirm again with Apple. The server verifies and revokes Apple authorization before deleting the account. Canceling that confirmation leaves the account intact.

Deletion removes the active cloud account, profile, meal history/sync markers and usage records, requests RevenueCat profile removal, and clears that device’s account cache. Separate guest logs and other devices are not remotely erased. Provider backups/security records and legally required store transactions may remain.

Older TestFlight build 6 requires manually removing Calorie Pocket in your Apple Account’s Sign in with Apple settings after deletion.

If you cannot sign in, contact fabrod035@gmail.com. We may verify ownership. Do not send passwords or sensitive identity/medical documents.
