Terms of service

Adult nutrition log

Calorie Pocket is provided by Real Scripture Path Inc. Manual logging, goals, daily history, accounts, sync and sharing are free. Review portions and nutrition labels. Targets and reports are general estimates, not medical advice. The app is not intended for minors, pregnancy, eating-disorder treatment or medical nutrition therapy.

Accounts and backup

New iOS accounts use Sign in with Apple. Existing email accounts can sign in; email signup/reset delivery is not offered in the reports release. Keep credentials private. Sync requires a connection; verify sync status before relying on a backup. Guest entries are separate until you import them. Clearing storage may delete unsynced changes.

Optional Pro membership

Monthly and yearly auto-renewable memberships provide the same 7- and 30-day nutrition reports, daily charts and previous-period comparisons. Reports reflect your saved entries; missing days are not zero intake and partial logging can understate averages. Sample reports are labelled fictional. The reports release does not include photo AI.

The app displays each store’s actual localized total price and billing period before purchase. The store charges on confirmation. Membership renews automatically unless cancelled before the store’s renewal deadline. Manage/cancel renewal in Account or store settings. Restore purchases using your original Calorie Pocket account. Refunds follow store policies. Deleting your account or app does not cancel renewal.

Sharing
