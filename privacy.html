Privacy policy

This policy describes the updated nutrition-reports release. An older TestFlight build has additional beta handling explained below.

Your log and account

Guest meals and goals stay on your device. New iOS accounts use Sign in with Apple; we receive an account identifier and, when permitted, your name and email or private relay address. We never receive your Apple password. Existing email accounts can still sign in. Email signup and reset delivery are not offered in the reports release.

Supabase provides authentication and stores signed-in profile details (adult age, sex, height/weight and activity), goals, meal names, nutrition values, notes and minimal deletion/sync markers. An isolated local cache supports offline changes. Signed-in account data is restricted to its owner.

Reports and purchases

Report calculations run on your device from saved entries. Apple or Google processes payments. RevenueCat receives a pseudonymous app account identifier, purchase history, membership entitlement and related operational information to validate access and restore purchases. We do not receive card details. No advertising SDK or cross-app advertising tracking is implemented.

Photos

The reports release does not request camera/photo-library permission, upload meal photos, or call a photo-AI provider.

Older TestFlight build 6 only

Invitation-only build 6 can optionally transmit a selected food photo through Supabase to Google Gemini after explicit consent. That beta uses unpaid Gemini quota: Google may use images/responses for improvement and human review. Do not send personal, sensitive or confidential information. The app/backend does not save those photos in meal history. See Google’s terms. These beta photo features are not offered in the new reports release.

Sharing and support

Summaries are previewed before sharing. Totals are included by default; meal names and goals require opt-in. Email, age/height/weight, notes and photos are excluded. You select the receiving app/person, whose policies govern shared copies. Support emails sent to fabrod035@gmail.com are used to handle requests; do not send passwords or medical records.

Retention and deletion

Guest data remains until removed or app storage is cleared. Cloud data is retained while your account is active. Signing out leaves an isolated local account cache. Deleted meals retain only minimal sync markers to stop old copies restoring them.

In the updated reports release, Apple users confirm again with Apple before deletion. The backend verifies and revokes Apple authorization, removes the Supabase account and cloud nutrition data, requests deletion of the RevenueCat profile, and clears the account cache on that device. Other devices and separate guest logs are not remotely wiped. Older TestFlight build 6 instead instructs manual Apple-authorization removal.

Provider backups, security/audit logs, support records and legally required store transactions may remain under provider retention/legal policies. We do not promise immediate erasure from every backup or shared copy. Account deletion does not cancel store renewal.

Your choices and security

Use guest logging, choose when to sync or share, remove entries, or use Account → Delete account. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or export data, restrict processing or complain to a privacy regulator. Contact fabrod035@gmail.com; we may verify ownership.

Hosted requests use HTTPS; native sessions use secure device storage. Hosting/authentication providers may retain technical request/security metadata. No system can guarantee absolute security. This app is intended for adults 18+, not medical therapy.

Contact and changes
