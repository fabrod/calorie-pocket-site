Who operates the app

Calorie Pocket is provided by Real Scripture Path Inc. This policy covers the Calorie Pocket mobile app, its nutrition backend, and this support website. Contact us about privacy at fabrod035@gmail.com.

Data depends on the features you choose.

Food photos and third-party AI

Photo analysis is optional and uses Google Gemini. Our backend sends the selected image and a food-analysis prompt; it does not include your account email, saved profile, or other meal history in that Gemini request. Personal information visible inside an image would still be transmitted, so use food-only photos without faces, documents, addresses, or medical details.

Our application database does not store meal photos, and our analysis code does not intentionally log photo payloads. Your selected image may remain in temporary device storage or your own photo library. Google processes and may retain inputs and outputs under its own service terms; deleting an app entry does not erase a photo from your library or every provider record.

Current beta configuration: Gemini uses unpaid API quota. Under Google’s unpaid-service terms, submitted content and responses may be used to improve Google products and may be reviewed by humans. Do not submit sensitive, confidential, or personal information. You may skip photo analysis and use manual logging.

Read Google’s Gemini API terms and Google’s privacy policy for provider handling. We will update this policy if the processing arrangement changes.

Service providers and sharing

We use providers to operate the features you request:

  • Supabase: account authentication, cloud nutrition storage, and server functions. The current app backend is hosted in the United States.
  • RevenueCat and the applicable app store: subscription validation, purchase restoration, and entitlement management.
  • Google Gemini: optional food-photo estimates.
  • Render: hosting this public website and processing normal website connection requests.
  • Email services: support correspondence and account confirmation/recovery messages when configured. Resend is the intended transactional email provider; public email delivery is still being configured for the beta.

Providers may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate, subject to their applicable terms and safeguards. Their policies are available from Supabase, RevenueCat, Render, and Resend.

We do not sell your personal information or use nutrition data for targeted advertising. This website contains no advertising pixels or analytics scripts added by us. Providers may process essential operational/security information.

We may disclose information when required by law, to protect security or legal rights, or as part of a lawful business transfer with appropriate privacy protections.

Sharing a daily summary

Sharing begins only when you choose it. The app previews the image or text before opening your device’s share sheet. Totals are included by default; meal names and daily goals require opt-in. The shared summary excludes account identifiers, profile age/height/weight, meal notes, and photos. You select the receiving app or person. Their handling and audience settings are outside our control.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Guest logs remain on your device until you remove them or clear app storage. Signed-in nutrition data is kept while your account is active so you can use history and sync. Signing out retains that account’s isolated offline cache on that device; it does not delete the cloud account. Clearing storage can permanently remove unsynced changes.

Deleted meal contents are replaced with minimal synchronization markers, retained with the account to prevent older device copies from restoring the meal. Account deletion removes the active Supabase account, cloud profile, meals/markers, and usage records, requests removal of the RevenueCat customer profile, and clears the account cache on the device performing deletion. Separate guest logs and copies on other devices are not remotely erased.

Provider backups, security/audit records, correspondence needed to resolve a request, and store transaction records may remain for the period required by the applicable provider or law. We do not promise immediate removal from every backup, third-party system, or recipient’s shared copy.

Use Account → Delete account in the app. See the account-deletion page for steps and help if you cannot sign in. Account deletion does not cancel a store subscription. Apple’s separate sign-in authorization currently requires manual removal in Apple settings after deletion.

Your choices and requests

You can use free manual logging without an account, decline camera access, avoid AI analysis, choose what to share, edit/delete entries, or delete your account. Stop new AI transmission by not requesting analysis. Stop account syncing by signing out; use account deletion if you want the cloud record removed.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your information, restrict/object to processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a privacy regulator. Email fabrod035@gmail.com. We may need to verify account ownership before acting. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, medical records, or identity documents unless a secure, necessary verification process has been agreed.

Security and adults only

We use HTTPS for hosted app requests, account-based database access controls, and secure device storage for native authentication sessions. These measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security. Protect your device and credentials.

Calorie Pocket is intended for adults aged 18 and older. It is not directed to children and is not for pregnancy, eating-disorder treatment, or medical nutrition therapy. Contact us if you believe a child has supplied account information.
