Support

Help with your account, history, reports or membership.

Email fabrod035@gmail.com. Include your app version and a description. Never send passwords, payment-card details or medical records.

Start free

Manual logging, goals, daily history, accounts, cloud sync and sharing are free. Use Sign in with Apple for a new iOS account; existing email accounts can sign in. Email signup/reset delivery is not offered in this release. Guest logs stay on the device until explicitly imported.

Reports and Pro

Pro memberships unlock 7- and 30-day reports, daily charts and comparisons. The sample preview uses fictional data. Real reports use your saved entries, so partial logs affect averages. Photo AI is not included in the reports release.

Sign in to the original Calorie Pocket account to restore purchases. Manage/cancel renewal through Account or Apple subscription settings. The store handles billing/refunds; account deletion does not cancel renewal.

Sync and deletion

Check sync status before changing devices. Offline changes wait on that device. Clearing app data may delete unsynced work. Delete your account from Account; see deletion instructions and privacy details.
