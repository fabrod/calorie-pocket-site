Common questions

Can I use it without paying or signing in?

Yes. Manual calorie/protein logging and general adult goals work as a local guest. Accounts add free cloud history; Pro is for optional photo analysis.

My guest meals did not appear after sign-in.

Guest history stays separate. In Account, choose Import this device’s guest log and review the confirmation. Signing in does not upload guest meals automatically.

My meals are not syncing.

Check the account shown, internet access, and sync status under Account, then try Sync now. Keep the app’s data until pending changes finish. On another device, use the same Calorie Pocket account.

How do I restore or cancel Pro?

Sign in to the original app account and choose Restore purchases in Account. Use Manage / cancel for store subscription settings. For Apple billing or refunds, use Apple billing support or Apple’s refund request site. Deleting the app/account does not cancel renewal.

Why did an AI estimate fail or look wrong?

Check Pro status, connectivity, and usage limits. Try a clear food-only photo and verify portions. Accepted failures and no-food results count toward the disclosed limit. You can always enter nutrition manually. AI estimates are approximate.

Where is my TestFlight invitation?

Invitation-only beta access requires the email that was invited. Open Apple’s invitation email on the iPhone and choose View in TestFlight. Check spam, install TestFlight, and contact us if the invitation is missing or expired.

What if signup or reset emails do not arrive?

Check the address and spam folder. Open the link on the device that requested it. Public email delivery is still being configured for this beta; native iOS Sign in with Apple does not require our confirmation email. Contact support rather than repeatedly creating accounts.
