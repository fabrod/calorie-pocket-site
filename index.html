A clearer picture of your nutrition

Log meals, track calories and protein, and follow your own daily goals. Review your history, sync across devices, and choose what to share.

Start free. Stay in control.

Manual logging, adult goals, daily history, accounts, cloud sync and summary sharing are free. You can keep a guest log on your device without an account.

A wider view with Calorie Pocket Pro

Optional monthly and yearly memberships include 7- and 30-day nutrition reports, daily charts and comparisons with the previous period. Both plans include the same reporting features. View the actual localized prices and billing period in the app before purchasing.

Reports use your saved entries. Missing days are not treated as zero intake; partial logs can understate intake. A clearly labelled sample preview helps you explore the reports.

The reports release does not include photo AI.

Review, then share.

Preview an image or text summary before choosing an app and recipient. Meal names and goals require opt-in; nothing is posted automatically.

For adults 18+. General nutrition estimates are not medical advice. The updated reports release is being prepared for review; public App Store availability will be announced after release.
