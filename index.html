01 / KEEP IT SIMPLE

A log that fits your day.

Track calories and protein, calculate general adult goals, and look back through daily history. Manual logging is free.

02 / YOUR CHOICE

Local first. Sync if you like.

Keep a guest log on your device, or create an account for cloud history. Importing your guest log is always your choice.

03 / REVIEW, THEN SAVE

A helpful photo estimate.

Optional Pro photo analysis uses Google Gemini to suggest food and nutrition. Check portions and correct every estimate before saving.

A little more, with Pro

Monthly or yearly. The same Pro features.

Calorie Pocket Pro includes up to 100 accepted photo estimates per UTC calendar month, with a limit of 5 per rolling minute. Failed analyses and no-food results count after a request is accepted. Current prices and billing periods appear in the app before purchase.

Manual logging, goals, account sync, and daily-summary sharing remain free. Subscriptions renew unless cancelled through the store.
